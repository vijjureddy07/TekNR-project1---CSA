About Pure Fields

The mission and people behind our weekly harvest boxes.

Pure Fields was built to make local food feel dependable, not occasional. We work with nearby growers, pack to seasonal strength, and keep the subscription simple enough for real weekly life.

What started as a small direct-to-neighbor produce route has grown into a local CSA program built around freshness, flexible subscriptions, and long-term relationships with regional farms. We focus on strong field picks, low-waste packing, and produce that lands in member kitchens fast enough to still feel alive.

Growing standards

Quality is built in the field, not added later in the supply chain.

We source from farms that prioritize soil health, seasonal timing, and harvest freshness. That means better flavor, fewer weak substitutions, and produce that holds up in a real kitchen.

Soil-first growing

We prefer farms using low-spray, regenerative, and soil-building practices over short-term high-yield shortcuts.

Clean packing routes

Boxes are packed close to delivery windows so members get better shelf life and less handling than typical retail produce.

Local first sourcing

Whenever possible, we keep the box rooted in nearby farms so the membership supports the surrounding food system directly.

Transparent standards

Members should understand why the box changes by season, how substitutions work, and what freshness tradeoffs actually look like.
