Plan overview

Built for families who want a dependable weekly mix.

The Family Box is the most balanced plan in the lineup. It works well for shared dinners, mixed eaters, and members who want a solid produce baseline before adding eggs, bread, or pantry extras.

Farm source / freshness info

Packed from a rotating network of nearby growers.

The Family Box is sourced from partner farms that use low-spray, regenerative, and soil-first growing practices. The pack mix shifts by field strength, route timing, and what is at peak quality that week.

Most produce is harvested within a day of packing, cooled quickly, and staged in reusable boxes so members receive better texture and shelf life than typical retail handling.

FAQ

Questions members usually ask before they start this plan.

Answers cover substitutions, missed deliveries, billing, allergies, and plan changes.

Can I request substitutions in the Family Box?

Yes. Members can flag dislikes and request eligible swaps before the weekly cutoff if alternate inventory is available.

What happens if I miss a delivery?

Delivery notes, pickup switches, and address changes can be managed in the dashboard ahead of route day. If a route is missed, support can review the order history and route notes.

How does billing work after the first order?

Your card is billed automatically on the schedule attached to your membership, and renewals continue until you pause or cancel.

Can you accommodate allergies?

You can note common produce dislikes and certain sensitivities, but because packing happens in a farm-box system, ingredient-specific guarantees should be confirmed with support before subscribing.

Can I change from Family Box to another plan later?

Yes. Plan changes can be made from the dashboard before the next billing cutoff, subject to inventory and route availability.
