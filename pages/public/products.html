FAQ

Questions members usually ask before subscribing.

The answers below cover box contents, substitutions, payments, delivery, and membership control.

What will be in my box?

We send a weekly preview before billing closes. The mix changes with the season, but box volume stays consistent with your selected plan.

Can I request substitutions?

Yes. When inventory allows, members can swap or flag dislikes in the dashboard before the weekly cutoff.

How do recurring payments work?

Your card is charged automatically on the cadence you select at sign-up, either weekly or monthly, until you pause or cancel.

What happens if I am not home for delivery?

You can leave delivery notes, switch to pickup, or update your address in advance. We also send route reminders on delivery day.

Can I pause my membership for a trip?

Yes. Use the dashboard to skip an individual week or pause a longer stretch without restarting your account later.
