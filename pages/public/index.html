How It Works

A simple subscription flow from plan selection to weekly pickup.

The main experience is built around the same system shown on the products page: choose a plan, manage the schedule, receive your harvest, and update everything from the dashboard when needed.

01

Choose the right box

Start with a Small, Family, Fruit, or Vegetable Box based on how your household actually cooks.

02

Set billing and delivery

Pick a weekly or biweekly cadence where available and choose delivery or a nearby pickup point.

03

Receive the harvest

We pack to seasonal strength so the box reflects what is freshest and strongest in the field that week.

04

Manage it in your dashboard

Skip a week, pause a membership, change your address, or renew without manual back-and-forth.

Recurring plans stay flexible, and add-ons can be attached later as your routine changes through the season.
