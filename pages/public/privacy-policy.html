Privacy Policy

How Pure Fields handles member data.

We only collect the information needed to manage memberships, deliveries, pickup coordination, billing, and customer support. This page explains what we collect and how it is used.

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you create an account, subscribe to a plan, update delivery details, contact support, or join the mailing list. This can include your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, billing information, and subscription preferences.

How We Use Information

We use your information to manage weekly subscriptions, confirm deliveries or pickups, process billing, respond to support requests, and send service-related updates. With your permission, we may also send seasonal product updates or farm news.

Delivery, Billing, And Support Records

  • Delivery instructions are used to complete weekly dropoffs and avoid missed boxes.
  • Billing records are used to process renewals, pauses, skips, and plan changes.
  • Support messages are kept so our team can follow up on issues and improve service quality.

Sharing And Retention

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who help us process payments, manage communication, or coordinate deliveries. We keep records only as long as needed for operations, legal compliance, and customer support.

Your Choices

You can request updates or corrections to your account information, opt out of marketing emails, or contact us about privacy questions at support@purefields.com.
