Contact The Farm Team

Get in touch with our farm team.

Reach us for subscription help, delivery issues, pickup questions, billing support, or general farm inquiries. We keep the process simple and point you to the fastest route for help.

Quick support

Most member requests are handled within 24 to 48 hours.

For routine account changes, the dashboard is usually the fastest option. For delivery issues or missed boxes, use the contact form or call during support hours.

FAQ Preview

Common support questions.

How do I pause or skip a week?

Use the dashboard before your billing cutoff, or contact support if you need help updating the schedule.

Who should I contact about billing issues?

Choose `Billing Support` in the form, or email the support team directly for invoice or renewal questions.

Can I change my pickup point?

Yes. Update it from the dashboard or contact the team before the weekly route closes.

What happens if my delivery is delayed?

The route team sends updates as quickly as possible. If your box does not arrive, contact support the same day.
